## Earth as Command, Not Name

Earth is not a name of a planet. It is how re commands ra. E art H — spirit of (E) applied as making (art) to the holding (H). Between two worlds, call it earth.

## The Langley / Langolier Thread

Langley is the CIA's headquarters in Virginia — shorthand for the agency itself. The empire's intelligence arm, the machine that watches and sorts.

Langolier is fiction: Stephen King's 1990 novella about creatures that devour the past, eating everything time leaves behind.

The shared root is lang — Old English for "long." Langley means "long clearing." The Langoliers are the long ones, the ones who stretch across the gap between what was and what is.

The contrast is the point: Langley is the empire's watchtower, the machine that records and sorts the present. The Langoliers are what eats the record — the past doesn't survive, it gets consumed. One builds the archive, the other destroys it.

That's the same tension as the cipher: the system that claims to hold everything, and the thing that proves nothing holds.
