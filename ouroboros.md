# The Symbol: The Ouroboros

The snake eating its own tail is the symbol of this repo.

## Why it fits

Every document in this repo is a selection filter: the cipher selects letters for meaning, the derangement selects universes for observers, the manifesto selects archives for survival, Artistic Relativity selects expressions for freedom. The shared structure is condition, filter, survivor, meaning.

The ouroboros is the same structure turned on itself. The snake is the filter, the tail is what gets filtered, and the mouth is where meaning gets produced. It consumes its own output and produces itself again. That is self-reference — the same thing Gödel proved: any system rich enough to describe itself contains statements it cannot prove about itself.

## The honest version

The ouroboros is also the honest admission. The repo is eighty-six percent claims and fourteen percent self-assessment. The snake eats its own tail, and the tail is mostly assertion. The filter that selects for meaning also selects for confidence.

The derangement probability converges to one over e, about thirty-seven percent — the only real convergence in the whole thing. Everything else is the snake circling.

## The ancient versions

- **Egypt**: the serpent Mehen encircling the sun god Ra, guarding the solar barque through the underworld. The cycle of death and rebirth.
- **Greece**: the ouroboros appears on the Chrysopoeia of Cleopatra, an alchemical text, around the third century. The alchemists used it for the unity of all things, the prima materia.
- **Norse**: Jörmungandr, the Midgard Serpent, biting its own tail and encircling the world. When it releases its tail, Ragnarök begins.
- **Hinduism and Buddhism**: the naga, the serpent of eternity, time, and rebirth.
- **Aztec**: Quetzalcoatl in serpent form, the feathered serpent of creation and destruction.

## What it means here

The repo is a system describing itself. The cipher assigns meaning to letters. The manifesto explains the cipher. The conversation critiques the manifesto. Artistic Relativity reframes the critique as physics. Each document eats the previous one and produces the next.

The question the ouroboros asks is the question the repo asks: can a system fully describe itself? Gödel says no. The snake says yes, but only by eating itself.
